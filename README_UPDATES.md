# 🎉 L'Oréal Routine Builder - New Features!

## What's New (Latest Update)

Your L'Oréal Routine Builder has been enhanced with three powerful new features:

### ✨ Feature 1: Product Search & Filter
Search products in real-time by name, brand, or keyword. The search box works seamlessly with the category filter for precise product discovery.

**How to use:**
- Type in the search box at the top
- Results update as you type
- Combine with category filter for better results

### 💬 Feature 2: Follow-Up Questions & Conversation Memory
After generating your routine, ask unlimited follow-up questions! The AI remembers your entire conversation and provides context-aware answers.

**How to use:**
1. Generate your routine by clicking the button
2. Ask questions like "Can I mix these products?" or "How often should I use this?"
3. The AI responds with full context from your routine

### 🎨 Feature 3: Enhanced Generate Button
The Generate button now features a beautiful L'Oréal brand gradient that transforms on hover, providing visual feedback and premium feel.

**Button Features:**
- Beautiful red gradient when enabled
- Transforms to gold on hover with lift effect
- Disabled state with helpful tooltip
- Smooth animations throughout

## Quick Start

1. **Select a Category** - Choose from Cleansers, Moisturizers, Makeup, etc.
2. **Search (Optional)** - Use the search box to find specific products
3. **Select Products** - Click products to add them to your routine
4. **Generate Routine** - Click the stunning "Generate Routine" button
5. **Ask Questions** - Chat with the AI about your routine
6. **Keep Learning** - The AI remembers everything!
